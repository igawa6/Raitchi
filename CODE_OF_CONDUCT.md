# Code of Conduct

AC-NativeNX adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
The full text at that address is our Code of Conduct and applies to every repository, issue, pull request,
discussion and community space of the project, and to AI agents acting for contributors or for the maintainers.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is
best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, publishing other people's private
information, and sexualised language or imagery are not acceptable.

## Project-specific additions

- **Asking for, sharing or pointing to game files, keys or firmware** is treated as a conduct violation,
  in addition to being against [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Upstream authors are credited, not erased.** Removing or rewording someone's copyright notice or
  credit without their agreement is a violation.
- AI agents follow the same rules as people and disclose themselves (`Agent: <name> · Model: <id>`).
- Pressuring maintainers or upstream authors for release dates, builds or support is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately through GitHub: open a private security advisory from the
**Security** tab of this repository ("Report a vulnerability") and say it is a conduct report, or, for
something that is not sensitive, open an issue. Do not include game content. Reports are handled
confidentially where possible. Maintainers apply the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
